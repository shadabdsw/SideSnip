Keep the text and files you reuse in a glass panel at the side of your browser. Click to copy, or insert straight into any form.

Chrome, Edge, Brave or another browser
  1. Download SideSnip and unzip it to a folder you’ll keep.
  2. Open your browser’s Extensions page, for example chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder with manifest.json in it.

What it does

  • One click - Click a snippet to copy it, or press ↵ to type it straight into the field you’re on.

  • Save what you copied - Turn the text you just copied into a snippet by giving it a title.

  • Files too - Keep your resume or images and attach them to any upload field.

  • Groups - Sort snippets into groups like Work and General, and search across all of them.

  • Your layout - Float it on either side or dock it to a corner, in light or dark.

  • Private - No account and no tracking; it only appears on sites you turn it on for.
